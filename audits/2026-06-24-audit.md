# Autoresearch Audit — 2026-06-24

A consolidated audit of everything attempted across the four autoresearch runs
(`jun1`, `jun2`, `jun4`, `jun5`) plus the 2026-06-23 metric revision, with findings
and concrete recommendations for the next round of work.

Scope reviewed: `attempts/{jun1,jun2,jun4,jun5}/` (results.tsv, LEARNINGS.md,
SUMMARY.md, per-iter snapshots), `results.tsv`, `docs/{ANNOTATION_AMBIGUITY,
METRIC_VALIDATION,2026-06-23-eval-system-summary}.md`, `logs/`, the current
`annotation_pipeline.py`, `tools/`, and `IDEAS.md`.

---

## 1. The task & harness (recap)

Given one pharmacogenomics paper's `markdown_content`, predict
`{"variant_sentences": {variant -> [standardized PharmGKB association sentences]}}`.
Only `annotation_pipeline.py` is editable; `eval.py`/`generate.py` are fixed.
Generation and scoring are decoupled (so generations can be re-scored without
re-paying generation cost — this turned out to be load-bearing during the metric
revision when the Anthropic balance was exhausted).

**Both primary metrics are recall-only** (extra predictions never penalized):
- `meaning_capture` (PRIMARY) — since 2026-06-23, **paper-level &
  representation-invariant**: pool all distinct gold sentences, score against the
  whole predicted pool, ignore variant keys. Judge stays strict on
  direction/polarity/phenotype.
- `variant_coverage` (PRIMARY) — gold-key recall with rsID↔star equivalence.

⚠️ **Comparability warning that governs this whole audit:** the harness changed
*three times*. (a) jun1 used a per-sentence judge (`sentence_coverage`). (b) jun2
used an early `meaning_capture`. (c) jun4/jun5 used the per-variant batch judge —
**and per the project owner jun1/jun2/jun4 actually ran against a pre-commit
`eval.py` that differs from what's in the tree**, so only jun5's absolute numbers
are trustworthy on the pre-revision harness. (d) The 2026-06-23 revision changed
the primary `meaning_capture` definition again. **Absolute scores are NOT
comparable across runs.** What carries across runs is the *qualitative lessons*,
which are remarkably consistent.

## 2. Run-by-run summary

### jun1 — per-sentence judge era (model: gpt-4o-mini → gpt-5.4)
Metric was `sentence_coverage` (micro). Explored aggressive recall: exhaustive
3-category prompt, reciprocal/genotype enumeration, 2-pass/3-pass ensembles,
few-shot, HLA decomposition, regex variant injection.
- Best `sentence_coverage` ≈ **0.865** (iter8: direction-optional + phenotype
  grouping, 2422 predicted sentences); best `variant_coverage` **0.831**
  (iter15: regex variant backstop, output-only).
- Lessons that survived: the 2nd reader pass mattered *on this micro metric*;
  injecting the regex inventory into the **prompt** hurt (iter14) but using it as
  an **output-only backstop** helped coverage (iter15); over-inflating volume
  (3082 sentences, iter12) dropped val — judge confusion.

### jun2 — early meaning_capture (model: gpt-5.4)
Introduced cross-filing and `*1` handling.
- Best **0.675** (iter13: gene-context cross-filing + acronym-hijack bugfix +
  cap per-variant candidates at 60), coverage **0.899**.
- Lessons: additive wild-type→`*1` cross-filing recovers reference-allele keys
  (iter6); 3rd reader pass and model-diversity ensembles *diluted* the judge;
  capping per-variant candidates *helped* (first sign the macro punishes volume).

### jun4 — batch per-variant judge (model: gpt-5.4) — **the pivot run**
- Champion **iter7 (`d5528b7`): 0.467 / 0.899** — single rich PharmGKB-convention
  pass with **model-side cross-filing** (let the model decide which alleles a
  sentence belongs under, incl. `*1`), no post-processing.
- Two decisive findings:
  1. **The batch judge punishes *adding* content.** Every recall tactic that
     raised micro `sentence_coverage` *lowered* macro: exhaustive flooding (0.392),
     **regex** cross-filing (0.403, even though sentence_cov jumped +0.09), 2-pass
     union (0.439), cap=3 (0.407). 74/90 dev variants have exactly 1 gold sentence,
     so extra sentences dilute the single-sentence variants that dominate the macro.
  2. **The metric is noise-dominated at 16 papers** (identical baseline 0.448 →
     0.412). Validate candidates with a 2nd generation.

### jun5 — same harness, **model swap** (model: gpt-5.4 → claude-opus-4-8)
- Champion **iter2 (`8abb30e`): 0.558 mean** (0.536 / 0.598 / 0.540 over 3 runs),
  coverage ~0.90. Net **+0.14 macro over the gpt-5.4 baseline (0.414)**.
- Three findings that define the current understanding of the task:
  1. **Model choice is the dominant lever.** gpt-5.4 → opus-4-8 on the *identical*
     prompt was **+0.12**, dwarfing every prompt/structure tweak (±0.03–0.05).
     gemini-2.5-pro (0.503) also ≫ gpt-5.4 (0.418); opus edged gemini.
  2. **It's a model×prompt INTERACTION, not the model alone.** The ablation (iter6)
     is the key result:

     |          | minimal prompt | rich PharmGKB prompt |
     |----------|----------------|----------------------|
     | gpt-5.4  | 0.414          | 0.418                |
     | opus-4-8 | 0.409          | **0.536**            |

     The rich prompt buys opus +0.13 and gpt-5.4 ~0. You need **both**: opus can
     actually *execute* the conventions gpt-5.4 received identically and ignored.
  3. **Adding content/compute never helped the macro** (4th–6th confirmations):
     extended thinking (−0.08), dev-driven framing rules (flat), few-shot (−0.04),
     opus+gemini fill-missing ensemble (flat, **zero** coverage gain).
- Noise is wider on opus (~±0.05–0.06); iter5/7/8 were all *within* the champion
  band, not real regressions.

### 2026-06-23 — metric revision (no pipeline change; `eval.py` rewritten)
Driven by `ANNOTATION_AMBIGUITY.md`: the old per-key metric conflated *extraction
skill* with *PharmGKB filing convention*. A correct association filed under a
different-but-valid key scored 0; one biological finding cross-filed under up to 15
keys was charged as 15 obligations (PMC6435416).
- New primary `meaning_capture` = paper-level, representation-invariant; new
  `variant_coverage` = rsID↔star equivalence (curated single-defining-SNP table,
  bipartite matching).
- **Re-baseline verdict:** a genuine improvement *for fairness, not precision*.
  - Re-scored champion-era runs: mean **0.548** (new) vs 0.536 (old per-key).
  - Fresh 3-run regeneration (2026-06-24, key restored): **0.485** mean
    (0.558 / 0.455 / 0.443), coverage **0.891**.
  - All 6 champion runs mean **0.517** — new and old agree in level (inside noise),
    so switching the target loses nothing, while the new metric correctly credits
    biology the old one zeroed (misfiled-key synthetic test: 1.000 new vs 0.000 old).
  - **Caveat:** paper-level pooling reintroduces *volume sensitivity* via judge
    distractor-confusion — a 155-sentence flooder scored a present meaning 0.00.
    Only bites flooders; clean pools (~8) unaffected. Read `meaning_capture`
    alongside pool size.

## 3. Cross-cutting findings (what we actually know)

1. **Model quality is the single biggest lever found.** +0.12 from one model swap
   vs ±0.05 for every prompt idea combined. The task is model-bound at the top.
2. **…but only in interaction with the rich convention prompt.** Neither alone gets
   there; opus + rich prompt is the sweet spot. This is the most important and
   least obvious finding.
3. **"Better, not more."** Across *three* harnesses and ~6 explicit tests, adding
   content/compute (2nd/3rd pass, ensembles, thinking, regex cross-file, few-shot,
   exhaustive enumeration) never raised the meaning macro and usually lowered it.
   The judge rewards a tight, faithful, single-pass list. **This is the most
   robustly replicated lesson in the project — stop re-testing it.**
4. **Variant coverage is effectively maxed (~0.90).** The missing ~10% is hard for
   *both* opus and gemini (the ensemble recovered **zero** new gold variants) →
   it's not a model blind spot but likely genuinely-ambiguous or
   beyond-the-markdown (supplement) gold (ambiguity doc item 11).
5. **Everything is noise-limited at N=16.** Judge noise ±0.05–0.06; identical code
   spans 0.44–0.59. Any single-run delta < ~0.06 is invisible. **≥3 generations
   mean±std is mandatory** and has not yet been institutionalized in the loop.
6. **Half the historical "score gap" was the metric, not the model.** The
   ambiguity audit showed items 1–5/10–11 (key granularity, cross-filing, `*1`,
   rsID↔star, phenotype renaming, supplements) are convention/representation tests,
   not extraction tests. The 2026-06-23 revision fixed items 1–4. The remaining
   meaning gap is now a cleaner signal of real extraction skill.

## 4. Where the score actually stands

- Current champion = the committed pipeline: **single opus-4-8 call + rich PharmGKB
  prompt**. On the **new** primary metric: `meaning_capture` ≈ **0.49–0.55**,
  `variant_coverage` ≈ **0.89–0.91** (mean of 3 fresh regenerations: 0.485 / 0.891).
- So coverage is near-ceiling; the open frontier is the **~45–50% of pooled gold
  *meaning* still unrecovered per paper.**

## 5. What's been ruled out (don't repeat)

- ❌ More passes / ensembles / cross-model fill-missing (jun1 iter5/10, jun2
  iter7/10, jun4 iter9, jun5 iter8) — dilute the judge, 2× cost, zero coverage gain.
- ❌ Extended thinking / high reasoning_effort on opus (jun5 iter4) — −0.08.
- ❌ Few-shot worked examples (jun1 iter6, jun5 iter7) — narrows output, −0.04.
- ❌ **Regex** cross-filing as post-processing (jun4 iter4) — dilutes single-sentence
  variants. (Model-side cross-filing in the prompt is strictly better.)
- ❌ Injecting the regex variant inventory into the **prompt** (jun1 iter14) —
  panel sentences confused the judge. (Output-only backstop was mildly positive but
  coverage is now maxed anyway.)
- ❌ Exhaustive/reciprocal/genotype-enumeration flooding (jun1–jun4) — and now
  *doubly* discouraged: the new paper-level judge gets *distractor-confused* by huge
  pools (155-sentence pool → 0.00 on a present meaning).
- ❌ Swapping among frontier models for gains (jun5 iter3) — gemini < opus; the
  model lever is "use a frontier model," already banked.

## 6. Meaning-gap diagnostic (built & run 2026-06-24)

Recommendation A from the first draft of this audit is now **done**. New tool
`tools/diag_new_metric.py` reuses eval.py's exact pooling/judge path and, for each
**dev** paper, scores every distinct gold sentence and buckets each miss by cause
(stage 1 judge), then checks not-predicted golds against the paper markdown to split
*recoverable* from *unrecoverable* (stage 2 judge). Ran on a fresh champion dev
generation (`results/dev-champion-20260624-010328`, opus + rich prompt, verified
star-allele framing). Per-miss detail: `scratchpad/dev_diag_20260624.jsonl`.

**Result (dev, 88 distinct gold sentences, pass ≥ 0.9):**

| | count | % of misses |
|---|---|---|
| captured (≥ 0.9) | 66 / 88 (75%) | — |
| **MISSED** | **22 / 88 (25%)** | — |
| → not_predicted **/ recoverable** | 9 | 41% |
| → not_predicted **/ unrecoverable** (not in markdown) | 6 | 27% |
| → qualifier_missing (partial) | 4 | 18% |
| → phenotype_mismatch (vocab) | 2 | 9% |
| → polarity_direction (real bug) | 1 | 5% |
| **Rollup: FIXABLE** (recall/extraction) | **14** | **64%** |
| Rollup: VOCAB (phenotype renaming) | 2 | 9% |
| Rollup: UNRECOVERABLE (markdown gap) | 6 | 27% |

(Dev judge ≈ 0.63 vs val ≈ 0.49–0.55 — different papers + a custom categorizing
prompt; read the *bucket structure*, not the absolute level.)

### What the misses actually are (validated by reading every one)

1. **rsID nucleotide-genotype framing is the dominant fixable miss.** Almost every
   recoverable miss and every qualifier_missing is a gold sentence written as a
   **nucleotide-genotype contrast** for an **rsID** variant — *"Genotypes CT + TT is
   associated with decreased activity … as compared to genotype CC"*, *"Genotype GG
   is associated with increased likelihood of Mucositis…"*, *"Allele T … as compared
   to allele A"*. The champion prompt's blanket rule *"never use nucleotide genotypes
   (AA/GA), translate to alleles"* is **correct for star genes but wrong for plain
   rsIDs**, where gold expresses the association precisely as a genotype/allele
   contrast. The model drops these. This is exactly jun5's diagnosed top miss class —
   but jun5 tested a fix on the *old per-key* metric (flat, within noise); on the
   *new recall-only paper-level* metric capturing them is pure gain.
2. **Negative / absent HLA associations are under-reported.** PMC3839910's three
   misses are all *"HLA-X is **not** associated …"* findings the paper states as
   absence (*"none of the patients carried HLA-B\*15:02"*). The model reports positive
   HLA hits but omits the explicit negatives.
3. **~27% is genuinely unrecoverable** — TYMS/VNTR long-repeat genotypes, allele
   contrasts, and warfarin genotype rows that **do not appear in `markdown_content`**
   (supplement-only). This is a real ceiling (ambiguity-doc item 11): even a perfect
   extractor tops out around ~0.85–0.90 on dev. Should be reported as a ceiling, not
   chased.
4. **Phenotype vocab is a *minor* bottleneck (9%, both from one paper)** and
   **polarity is essentially a non-issue (1 case).** This **down-weights** the
   earlier draft's recommendation C — term normalization is not where the gap lives.

### The reframe this forces

The jun4/jun5 mantra *"adding content always hurts the macro"* was measured on the
**old per-key** metric, where extra sentences diluted single-sentence variant keys.
The **new metric is recall-only and pools by paper**, so adding the *missing
genotype-contrast and negative-HLA sentences* is **pure recall gain with no key
dilution** — as long as the pool stays far below the ~155-sentence judge-confusion
threshold (champion pools are ~10–13; huge headroom). **The metric change has
reopened the recall lever the prior runs closed.** That is the single most important
consequence of this diagnostic.

## 7. Recommendations going forward (revised by the diagnostic)

Ordered by expected value. The diagnostic moved **rsID genotype framing** to the top
and **demoted phenotype vocab**.

### A. ⭐ Fix rsID nucleotide-genotype & allele-contrast framing (the #1 lever now)
This is the largest fixable bucket (~9 recoverable + ~4 qualifier misses on dev) and
the prompt is *actively causing* it. The champion system prompt says, globally, *use
allele/diplotype framing, never nucleotide genotypes.* **Scope that rule to
star/HLA-allele genes only.** For plain rsID variants, instruct the model to express
associations as the gold does — *genotype contrasts* (`"Genotypes CT + TT is
associated with decreased … as compared to genotype CC"`) and *allele contrasts*
(`"Allele T … as compared to allele A"`), including the comparison group. Because the
new metric is recall-only and pooled by paper, these added sentences are **pure gain,
not dilution** (the lesson that blocked this on the old metric no longer applies).
Implement in `annotation_pipeline.py`, validate on dev with `tools/diag_new_metric.py`
(watch the `not_predicted/recoverable` + `qualifier_missing` buckets shrink), then
confirm on val over ≥3 generations. **Highest expected value, lowest cost.**

### B. ⭐ Emit negative / absent associations (esp. HLA)
Second fixable bucket. The model reports positive hits but omits *"X is **not**
associated …"* findings the paper states as an absence (*"none of the patients
carried HLA-B\*15:02"*). Add an explicit instruction to record reported negative
findings (non-associations / absent alleles) as `"is not associated"` sentences.
Strict-polarity judge means these must be phrased as genuine negatives. Validate the
same way as A.

### C. Audit & (if legitimate) fold in beyond-markdown gold — the ceiling
27% of dev misses are **unrecoverable from `markdown_content`** (supplement-only:
TYMS/VNTR repeats, warfarin genotype rows). `tools/fetch_supplements.py` /
`refresh_bench_supplements.py` exist. First: quantify and **report this as a ceiling**
(a perfect extractor tops out ~0.85–0.90 on dev). Second: check whether supplements
can be *legitimately* folded into the generation input via the bench-rebuild path —
if so it's the one lever that raises *both* metrics — but confirm it isn't changing
the scored target before relying on it.

### D. Institutionalize ≥3-generation mean±std reporting (process fix)
Make the loop's standard unit mean±std over ≥3 generations (ambiguity doc rec #6);
add a helper that runs 3 gens + 3 scores and prints mean/std. Noise is ±0.05–0.06, so
without this every A/B test < 0.06 is a coin flip (as iter5/7/8 showed). The
`diag_new_metric.py` bucket counts are a *lower-noise* signal than the headline score
for A and B — track them alongside.

### E. Lower-priority / opportunistic
- **Phenotype controlled-vocab normalization** — now demoted: only ~9% of misses (one
  paper, PMC10275785: "drug response" vs "disease activity/susceptibility"). **No term
  standardization is wired into the pipeline today** (the champion prompt tells the model
  to use *"the paper's terms"*), and the existing `tools/term_lookup.py`
  (`clinpgx-term-lookup`) exposes **only `DrugLookup` / `VariantLookup` — there is no
  ClinPGx phenotype/disease endpoint**, so `ClinPGxTermNorm` is *not* a usable phenotype
  source here. The only standardized phenotype vocabulary available is **local PharmGKB
  reference TSVs** (`base_data/variantAnnotations/var_*_ann.tsv`): a 20-value
  `Phenotype Category` set and 737 `Disease:<term>` values in `Population Phenotypes or
  diseases` (the specific outcome terms — "Recurrence", "MACE" → "Major Adverse Cardiac
  Events" — live in the `Sentence` column and would need mining). If pursued, build a
  phenotype term list from those TSVs and inject it into the prompt — but only after A/B.
- **Newer frontier models as they land** — model is the proven lever; a one-line swap
  is the cheapest high-ceiling experiment. Re-baseline over 3 gens.
- **Automated prompt search (GEPA/DSPy)** — noise-limited (each candidate needs ≥3-gen
  eval to beat ±0.06); only worth it once A/B are banked.
- **`*1` reference-allele handling** — the paper-level revision already removed most of
  its sting; don't spend pipeline effort here.
- **Watch pool size.** The new metric penalizes flooders via judge confusion (155-sent
  pool → 0.00). Champion pools are ~10–13 with huge headroom, but A/B will grow them —
  keep well under ~50 and re-check pool size if meaning_capture ever drops unexpectedly.

### What NOT to do
Don't re-run multi-pass / ensemble / thinking / regex-cross-file experiments — six
tests across three harnesses agree they don't help. **Caveat the old "adding content
hurts" lesson:** it was true on the *per-key* metric; on the new recall-only metric,
adding *genuinely missing gold meanings* (A, B) helps — what still hurts is adding
*redundant/contradictory* sentences or *flooding*. Don't tune toward PharmGKB filing
convention (cross-filing exactness, `*1` enumeration) for the *primary* metric.

## 8. One-line state of the world

A single **opus-4-8 + rich PharmGKB prompt** call is the champion (~0.49–0.55 meaning,
~0.90 coverage on the new metric). The 2026-06-24 dev diagnostic
(`tools/diag_new_metric.py`) shows **64% of the remaining meaning gap is fixable
recall** — dominated by **rsID nucleotide-genotype/allele-contrast framing the prompt
actively suppresses** and **omitted negative/HLA associations** — **27% is
unrecoverable supplement gold** (a real ceiling), and only **9% is phenotype vocab**.
The metric change reopened the recall lever the prior runs had closed: the next move
is to scope the "no genotypes" rule to star genes and emit negatives, validated by the
diagnostic's bucket counts over ≥3 generations.
